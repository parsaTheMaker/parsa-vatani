About

I build AI that works with physics, not around it.

I am a CFD/AI engineer, researcher, and lecturer working where mechanical engineering meets scientific machine learning.

My work moves between industrial vehicle development, large-scale aerodynamic datasets, learned simulation models, experimental energy systems, and the classroom. The goal stays consistent: make complex physical design faster to explore and easier to trust.
